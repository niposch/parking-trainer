# Parking proximity investigation — 18 October 2026

The old outer zones used seven rays inside a narrow fan. Thin obstacles could sit between rays, and obstacles alongside the doors or diagonally beside a rear corner could be outside every fan. The collision model still saw them, explaining contact without an active warning.

Modern parking systems can combine bumper sensing with lateral protection. [Bosch's parking-assist description](https://www.bosch-mobility.com/en/solutions/parking/parking-assist/) describes side-facing ultrasonic sensors scanning alongside the road; its [ultrasonic-sensor description](https://www.bosch-mobility.com/en/solutions/sensors/ultrasonic-sensor/) covers close-range obstacle sensing. The [SEAT example manual](https://www.seat.com/datamanual-manual/ateca/my18_w45/en-uk/ATECA_11_17_EN.pdf) describes front/rear and, with suitable equipment, side displays. Equipment and real acoustic detection limits vary; these are references, not identified specifications of the lesson car.

The prototype now calculates exact planar clearance to the same oriented rectangles used for the vehicle body and mirror collisions. Overlapping left/centre/right and front/rear regions eliminate sampling and handoff gaps; front outer zones include mirror clearance. Obstacles are clipped to the relevant side so an isolated left obstruction does not activate the right indicator. The minimum zone distances also drive the beeper. Low curbs remain excluded, as requested, while remaining physical obstacles.

The six existing stepped bumper indicators now also include lateral bars for the outer zones. Thresholds remain 1.6/1.0/0.6/0.3 m, with a 2 m maximum reported range. Approaching danger updates immediately; releasing a warning requires 4 cm additional clearance to avoid flicker. Detection is idealized geometric lateral protection, without ultrasonic propagation, material response, occlusion or motion-history reconstruction. It should not be described as calibrated OEM sensing.

Validation includes thin posts along the complete perimeter at four orientations, corner clearance, mirror-only contact, continuous wall approaches, 4,000 randomized rotated obstacles compared with an independent rectangle-edge distance calculation, and reverse-turn collision regressions. Running-game checks verify the resulting distances, independent console zones and continuous audio warning together.

## Distinct zones — 24 October 2026

The overlapping regions above made each outer bumper zone cover half of the body side, and the lateral bars reused the corner value, so rear-corner and side indicators were effectively identical. Proximity now uses ten zones that tile the plane around the car: front and rear left/centre/right bumper zones (corners wrap 0.5 m along the body; the centre spans ±0.40 m), plus front-left, front-right, rear-left and rear-right lateral zones split at the body centre. Each part of an obstacle belongs to the one zone it lies in and is measured to the complete body and mirror envelope. The nearest zone therefore remains the exact clearance, but an obstacle beside the doors lights only its lateral bar, and one behind a corner lights only that corner. Front-half lateral zones join the front beeper and rear-half zones the rear beeper. Checks place an obstacle in each zone and require every other zone to stay clear.

Run the focused suite with `dotnet run --project tests/Tests.csproj -- --proximity`.
